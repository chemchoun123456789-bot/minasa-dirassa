Privacy Policy

01 Overview

Your Privacy Is Fundamental

All user data is yours. Minassa Dirasa does not sell, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties under any circumstances. Your academic data exists solely to power your experience on the platform.

Minassa Dirasa ("the Platform," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and the rights you have over your data.

By creating an account and using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this document, you should discontinue use of the service.

When you use the Platform, standard technical logs may be generated by our hosting infrastructure (Supabase and Vercel). These may include IP address, browser type, and timestamps. These logs are used solely for security monitoring and are not linked to your profile data.

03 How We Use Data

Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is used for the following purposes only:

  • To create and maintain your account securely
  • To verify your identity through email OTP authentication
  • To synchronise your academic data across sessions and devices
  • To restore your settings and preferences on each login
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive account activity
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the technical infrastructure

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any commercial purpose. We do not share your data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

04 Storage & Security

How Your Data Is Protected

All user data is stored on Supabase, a SOC 2 Type II compliant cloud database platform. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and encrypted at rest within the database infrastructure.

OTP Authentication
Email verification required at registration. No password stored.
Rate Limiting
Maximum 5 OTP attempts before a 30-second lockout is enforced.
No Session Persistence
Sessions are not stored in browser cookies by default.
TLS Encryption
All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted.
Resend Cooldown
A 5-minute cooldown is enforced between OTP resend requests.
Isolated Profiles
Each user's data is isolated and inaccessible to other users.

06 Data Retention

How Long We Keep Your Data

Your account and all associated academic data are retained for as long as your account remains active. If you request account deletion, all personal data, including your name, email, school, and the full contents of your academic profile, will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Technical infrastructure logs are retained for a maximum of 90 days for security purposes, after which they are automatically purged.

You can delete your account yourself at any time by going to Settings and tapping Permanently Delete My Data. You may also request deletion by contacting the platform's developers on Telegram (t.me/bxlt3_m or t.me/elysiqn_xq), and this will be processed within 30 days.
07 Your Rights

Your Data Rights

Regardless of your location, we recognise the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right of access | You may request a complete export of all data associated with your account.
  • Right of rectification | You may correct inaccurate personal information directly within the application or by contacting us.
  • Right of erasure | You may delete your account and all associated data yourself at any time from Settings, or request permanent deletion by contacting us directly.
  • Right to portability | You may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON).
  • Right to restrict processing | You may request that we stop processing your data while a dispute is under review.
  • Right to object | You may object to any specific use of your data by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, send a written request to the contact address listed at the end of this document. We will respond within 30 days.

09 Service Description

What the Platform Provides

Minassa Dirasa is a free, browser-based academic productivity platform designed for students in Algeria and the wider Arabic-speaking world. The Platform provides the following tools, all at no cost:

  • Weekly class schedule builder with subject management
  • Pomodoro focus timer with session tracking and sound configuration
  • Task and assignment manager with priority tracking
  • Grade tracker with average calculation
  • Structured note-taking organised by subject
  • Exam schedule and countdown tracker
  • Study resource link manager

The Platform is provided "as is." We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any feature at any time without prior notice, though we will make reasonable efforts to inform users of material changes.
